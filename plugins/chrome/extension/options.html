Using it

  • The Relay button in the toolbar, or Alt+Shift+R, opens the side panel (change the shortcut).
  • In the panel, the page and camera buttons send the page you're on, or a screenshot of it, along with your question.
  • The scripts button in the panel: your scripts on Relay's Scripts page, run with arguments (the page's address or its selection, with a click), their output as it's written, Stop.
  • Right-click a page, a selection or a link: “Ask Relay about…”, “Summarize this page with Relay”, or “Run a Relay script with…” it as the argument.
  • Right-click a text field: “Rewrite with Relay” (improve, shorten, translate, or in your own words), then “Replace in field” under the answer. Any answer's “Insert in page” puts it where your cursor is.
  • Type relay, a space and your question in the address bar: the chat opens in Relay.
  • With Relay's chrome plugin on (Settings → Plugins in Relay), agents in any chat can read your tabs, click, type and open pages, where you're signed in, while Chrome runs. Each step shows first in the side panel (or a notification when it's closed): Deny, Allow, or Always on that site.
